# eval/

Metrics and figures.

- **Tracking error in simulation:** joint position error, body position and orientation error, per clip.
- **Isaac vs. MuJoCo:** how much tracking degrades in the second simulator.
- **Robustness:** fall rate under pushes and randomized physics.
- **Clip feasibility:** fraction of candidate clips the K1 can do, and why the rest fail.
- **Real robot:** videos and a qualitative comparison with simulation.
